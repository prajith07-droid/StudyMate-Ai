Time for a Brain Break!

You worked hard. Now give your mind some time to recharge.

🌿 Pause. Relax. Recharge.

A short break can help you return to your studies feeling fresh and focused.

💡 Choose Your Break

🚶

Take a Walk

Step away from your desk and take a short walk to refresh your mind.

💧

Hydrate

Drink some water and take a moment to relax before studying again.

🎵

Listen to Music

Enjoy a few minutes of your favorite music and clear your mind.

🌿

Relax

Sit comfortably, breathe slowly and give yourself a quiet moment.

👀

Rest Your Eyes

Look away from your screen and give your eyes a short rest.

🎨

Be Creative

Draw, sketch or do something creative for a few minutes.

⏰ 20-Minute Break Plan

0 - 5 min
💧 Drink water and relax
5 - 10 min
🚶 Walk around and stretch
10 - 15 min
🎵 Listen to music
15 - 20 min
🧘 Relax and prepare to study again
